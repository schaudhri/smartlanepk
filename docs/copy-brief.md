# SmartLane marketing copy brief (interview-led)

Source: founder interviews (Fatin, Adam, Umer). Use for `index.html`, `pages/*`, and `v2/` mirror.

## Pillars

1. **Pain**: No single view across couriers; different “out for delivery” meanings; cash stuck weeks; bulk sheets instead of per-order clarity; sale days + multiple couriers = stress; customers blame the brand.
2. **Outcome**: One dashboard for booking, tracking, and COD; know what you’re owed **per order**; fewer returns when delivery is reliable; **why** something failed, not only that it failed.
3. **Trust**: SmartLane stands behind COD regardless of which integrated courier delivers; funded company with a real balance sheet — say it plainly; proof via volume and tenure (verify numbers before publishing).
4. **Objections**: Cheapest rate vs sustainable service — consistency and transparency; “Does this help my bottom line?” — plain language and measurable outcomes.
5. **Positioning**: Not a courier, not a bank — aggregation + finance tied to COD; asset-light layer on a fragmented market.

## Headline formula

**Problem (implied) → relief → one proof**  
Example: Lead with visibility + money clarity + one screen; avoid empty “platform” claims without a plain noun after them.

## Trust snippets (use where space allows)

- “We’re not a courier — we connect you to many, and we stand behind your COD.”
- “Funded company with the balance sheet to back settlements — not a side project.”

## CTA hierarchy

- **Primary**: Book a demo (sales-led funnel).
- **Secondary**: Try for free → `https://app.smartlane.pk` (app handles signup/login).
