About SmartLane

Built inside the problem.

We are not a courier. We are not a bank. We are the technology layer that connects Pakistan's merchants, couriers, and capital — so merchants can focus on building their business.

Our story

SmartLane did not start in a boardroom. It started inside the same chaos you're probably dealing with right now.

Our founders spent years managing e-commerce brands in Pakistan — not building software for them, but actually running them. Five or six courier portals open at the same time. Payments stuck for 40 to 50 days. Bulk Excel sheets standing in for reconciliation. Customer complaints landing at the merchant's door when the courier was to blame. They lived it, and they solved it manually — routing orders, chasing settlements, negotiating rates — until the question became unavoidable: why isn't this a product?

What made SmartLane possible is that the team already understood both sides of the problem. Fatin and Adam had spent years inside Pakistani e-commerce — first helping build the country's earliest platforms, then running an agency that managed growing brands through exactly the operational chaos described above. Umer had spent his career on the other side: in investment banking and digital lending, trying to figure out how to get working capital to small businesses cheaply. The insight that brought all three together was simple: couriers already collect COD cash as part of their job. If you build the platform in the middle, the cost of financing goes to near zero. Logistics and cash flow aren't two separate problems — they never were. SmartLane is what happens when the people who lived the operations problem and the people who solved the financing problem build one product together.

That product connects your orders to the right courier automatically, settles your COD in hours instead of weeks, and gives you access to working capital based on your actual sales — not your credit history. Everything in one place, designed around how Pakistani merchants actually work.

We are not a courier. We are not a bank. We sit permanently in the middle — unbiased, connected, and always on your side. And because we built this from inside the problem, we know exactly which parts of your day we need to give back to you.

What we believe

The principles we build by.

We will never become a courier or a bank

Our value comes from sitting in the middle — unbiased, connected, and always on your side. We work with every courier so we can always recommend the right one for your order, not the one that's convenient for us.

Delivery and cash flow are one problem

A COD parcel is a product and a payment at the same time. Separating logistics from settlement was always the wrong model. We solve them together so you don't have to manage two systems.

The same tools, regardless of your size

Whether you ship 10 orders a month or 10,000, you get access to the same courier network, the same data, and the same financing options. No tiers. No minimums. No favourites.

Built for how you actually work

Pakistani e-commerce runs on COD, Instagram, and speed. Everything we build starts from that reality — not from what works in other markets.
