FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us →

How fast can we get started?
You can be up and running in under 30 minutes. Create an account, connect your store, and book your first shipment — no IT team or complex setup required.
Which couriers do you support?
SmartLane connects to 16+ couriers across Pakistan including TCS, Leopards, Trax, M&P, Rider, BlueEx, PostEx, and more. Compare rates and book from one dashboard.
Do you offer COD settlement visibility?
Yes. Smart Finance gives you a live view of your COD pipeline — courier-level settlement status, delay flags, and reconciliation history. All in one place.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — after your demo call we'll set you up with a 14-day free trial so you can connect your couriers and see SmartLane working with your actual orders before committing.
What does onboarding look like?
Our team guides you through the full setup — connecting your couriers, configuring your workflow, and training your ops team. Most merchants are fully live within one week.
Is there dedicated support after onboarding?
Yes. Every merchant has a dedicated account contact. We're available via WhatsApp, email, and phone during business hours, and we proactively flag issues before they affect your operations.
