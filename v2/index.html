FAQ

Common questions.

How fast can we get started?
You can be up and running in under 30 minutes. Create an account, connect your store, and book your first shipment — no IT team or complex setup required.
Which couriers do you support?
SmartLane connects to 16+ couriers — TCS, Leopards, Trax, M&P, BlueEx, PostEx, and others. The list evolves as we add partners; you always book and track from one dashboard.
Why not only use the cheapest courier rate?
Very low rates often don’t last — service drops when the promotion ends. We focus on steady delivery, clear tracking, and payouts you can plan around.
Who is responsible for my COD across couriers?
Your relationship is with SmartLane. We stand behind settlements for parcels through our integrated network — so you’re not chasing a new unknown operator every month.
Do you offer COD settlement visibility?
Yes. Smart Finance shows your COD pipeline order by order — collected, pending, settled — with delay flags and history in one place.
Can I track shipments in real time?
Yes — every shipment across all your couriers, tracked in one place. You'll see exceptions, delivery events, and NDRs as they happen — without logging into separate portals.
Is there dedicated support?
Yes. Our team is with you from onboarding through scaling. We help you configure your courier mix, understand your data, and get the most out of the platform.
