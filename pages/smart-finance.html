Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Smart Finance.

Can I export settlement statements?

Yes — export settlement statements for any courier, any date range. Ready for your accountant, your finance team, or an audit.

Does it work with multiple couriers?

Yes. Smart Finance pulls COD from every integrated courier into one view — so you’re not merging five statements by hand.

Who is responsible for my money?

SmartLane is your counterparty. We stand behind settlements for parcels that move through our integrated network — speak to us first, not a rotating list of small operators.

Is this only for big sellers?

No — if COD volume is growing and spreadsheets are breaking, you’ll feel the same pain at 200 or 2,000 orders a day. The product scales with you.
