Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the suite, pricing, and getting started.

Is there any setup fee?

No — there are no setup fees or hidden charges. You pay for what you use.

How long does onboarding take?

Most teams are shipping within 30 minutes of signing up. Our team guides you through connecting your store and first dispatch.

Can I start with one product and add more later?

Yes — SmartLane is modular. Start with Smart Delivery, add Smart Ops when you're ready, then layer in Smart Finance. No replatforming required.

Who holds my COD if I use several couriers?

Your relationship is with SmartLane. We stand behind settlements for parcels that move through our integrated network — so you’re not exposed to a new unknown operator every month.

Why not chase the lowest per-parcel rate?

Deep discounts often don’t last, and service slips when they end. We focus on steady delivery, clear tracking, and payouts you can plan around.
