Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Smart Ops.

Does Smart Ops work with my existing couriers?

Yes. Smart Ops works with all the courier partners SmartLane integrates with — TCS, Leopards, Trax, M&P, and more. Your orders, their courier statuses, one view.

Can I export reports?

Yes — export performance reports on demand or set a schedule. Send them directly to your team or leadership without manual work.

We’re on one courier only — is Smart Ops still useful?

If volume is low and nothing breaks, maybe not. Most teams here run multiple carriers or hit pain on sale days; that’s when a shared queue and alerts pay off.
